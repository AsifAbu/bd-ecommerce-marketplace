## Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines 2021 (issued 4 Jul 2021)

Main source: Bangla Tribune item-by-item summary. Key rules used in the plan:
- Hand order to courier within **48 h** of payment; deliver within **5 days** same city, **10 days** elsewhere.
- Refund within **10 days** if a paid order isn't delivered (the "7 days" figure was from the draft).
- Pay sellers within **10 days** after commission. Keep records **6 years**. Resolve complaints in **72 h**; appoint a compliance officer.
- Listings: measurements, material, colour, price, delivery charge, brand, model, country of origin, ingredients/risks, stock, images, return conditions. Seller name shown; marketplace keeps seller name, photo, NID, mobile, address. Seller holds trade licence, VAT, TIN, UBID or PRA number.
- Reviews can't be deleted; sellers and connected people can't review. Prior consent for personal data; disclose cookies.
- No expired, counterfeit or adulterated goods. Medicine needs a drug-administration (DGDA) licence. Terms must be in Bengali.
- Advance payment over 10% only via Bangladesh Bank escrow.

Other rules: **DBID** guideline 2022 (register, show DBID on site; obtainable with NID). **Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025** (approved 9 Oct 2025): specific consent, purpose/retention notices, breach reports, cross-border limits.

## Competitors

- **Daraz** (Alibaba): open marketplace. Search "processor" returned 2,434 results across 20 categories (food processors, guitar pedals) with irrelevant filters (size "EU M", colours, junk warranty values) and no socket/core filters. This is the problem our schema-driven catalog solves. 14-day returns (from Oct 2025). Pays: COD, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, cards, EMI. App rated 4.8.
- **Rokomari**: books-first, curated; approves every listing, barcodes stock, own Dhaka delivery (24 h), hotline 16297. Grew via content, loyalty and vouchers rather than discounts.
- Trust context: 58,720 complaints and about Tk 3,869 crore claimed against failed e-commerce platforms (Dhaka Tribune, 31 Aug 2026).
